Water Cloud Model (WCM) & Baghdadi Gates

Sentinel-1 SAR + S2 Optical

Decouples radar backscatter into vegetation ($\sigma^0_{veg}$) and attenuated soil ($\sigma^0_{soil}$) components to estimate surface soil moisture ($m_v\%$) with 10m interior buffering and speckle suppression.

$\sigma^0_{tot} = \sigma^0_{veg} + T^2 \cdot \sigma^0_{soil}$
$\sigma^0_{veg} = A \cdot \text{NDVI} \cdot \cos\theta \cdot (1 - T^2)$
$T^2 = \exp(-2 \cdot B \cdot \text{NDVI} \cdot \sec\theta)$
$m_v = D \cdot \sigma^0_{soil\text{ (dB)}} + C$
